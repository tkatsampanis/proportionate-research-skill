## Key Innovations in v3.0.0

- **Coverage category** — New classification between Required/Contextual that tracks which evidence dimensions have been investigated and which remain uncovered
- **Search Saturation** — Section 10 addition: track whether successive search rounds produce materially new evidence; 70–80% source overlap as heuristic signal (not automatic stop rule)
- **Novelty evaluation** — Section 24: assess whether further research likely produces new required claims, new evidence, contradictory evidence, better source quality, or unexplored coverage
- **Revised operating loop** — UNDERSTAND → CLASSIFY → PLAN → RETRIEVE → EVALUATE → UPDATE → VERIFY → STOP (added CLASSIFY and PLAN steps)
