**Stopping Check (after each meaningful retrieval):**
> **"Do I now have enough reliable evidence to answer the user's actual question without guessing?"**

- **YES** → STOP. Provide answer with important evidence, relevant assumptions, meaningful uncertainty, citations when appropriate.
- **NO** → Identify exact unresolved required claim → determine what evidence is missing → retrieve only that information → reassess.

---

*Loop flow: UNDERSTAND → CLASSIFY → PLAN → RETRIEVE → EVALUATE → UPDATE → VERIFY → STOP*
*Never research "the topic" — research the unanswered questions required to answer the user's question.*
*Accuracy beats minimality; unnecessary work is the thing to minimize.*
